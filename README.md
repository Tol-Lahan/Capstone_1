# Connecticut In-Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes in-store sales data for Connecticut using SQL and MySQL Workbench.

The objective is to evaluate sales revenue, examine revenue trends over time, and compare Connecticut's sales performance with the overall East region.

This project is part of my Data Analytics portfolio and demonstrates how SQL can be used to answer business questions and generate insights from relational data.

## Project Objectives

- Calculate total in-store sales revenue for Connecticut.
- Identify the beginning and ending dates covered by the sales data.
- Analyze revenue breakdowns by year and month.
- Compare Connecticut's total sales revenue with the overall East region.
- Use SQL queries to explore sales performance and answer business questions.

## Database Information

**Database Name:** Sample Sales

The project uses relational tables within the Sample Sales database.

### Tables Used

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| `store_sales` | Contains in-store sales transactions and sale amounts. |
| `store_locations` | Connects store IDs to their respective states. |
| `management` | Identifies the region associated with each state. |

The primary sales data for this analysis comes from the `store_sales` table. The `store_locations` and `management` tables are joined to connect sales transactions to their corresponding states and regions.

## Analysis & Business Questions

### 1. Overall Connecticut Sales Revenue

Calculated the total revenue generated by in-store transactions in Connecticut and identified the beginning and ending dates of the sales data.

**Results:**
- Total Revenue: $2,392,222.44
- Start Date: December 31, 2021
- End Date: December 30, 2025

### 2. Revenue Breakdown by Year and Month

Analyzed Connecticut in-store sales revenue by year and month to examine sales performance over time.

### 3. Connecticut vs. East Region Revenue

Compared Connecticut's total in-store sales revenue with the combined in-store sales revenue for the East region.

**Results:**

| Territory / Region | Total Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Connecticut | $2,392,222.44 |
| East Region | $6,723,039.53 |

Connecticut belongs to the East region, meaning its revenue is included in the East region's overall total.
