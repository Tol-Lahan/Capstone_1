# Connecticut In-Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes in-store sales data for Connecticut using SQL and MySQL Workbench.

The objective is to evaluate sales revenue, examine revenue trends over time, compare Connecticut's performance with the East region, and identify opportunities to improve sales.

This project is part of my Data Analytics portfolio and demonstrates how SQL can be used to answer business questions and generate insights from relational data.

## Project Objectives

- Calculate total in-store sales revenue for Connecticut.
- Identify the beginning and ending dates covered by the sales data.
- Analyze revenue breakdowns by year and month.
- Compare Connecticut's revenue with the overall East region.
- Examine transaction counts and average sales amounts by product category.
- Rank store performance within Connecticut.
- Provide recommendations based on the sales analysis.

## Database Information

**Database Name:** Sample Sales

The project uses relational tables within the Sample Sales database.

### Tables Used

| Table             | Description                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `store_sales`     | Contains in-store sales records and sale amounts.            |
| `store_locations` | Connects store IDs to their respective locations and states. |
| `management`      | Identifies the region associated with each state.            |
| `products`        | Contains product information and category IDs.               |

The primary sales data comes from `store_sales`. Other tables are joined to connect sales records to their corresponding stores, states, regions, and product categories.

## Analysis & Business Questions

### 1. Overall Connecticut Sales Revenue

Calculated the total revenue generated by in-store sales in Connecticut and identified the beginning and ending dates covered by the data.

**Results:**

- Total Revenue: **$2,392,222.44**
- Start Date: **December 31, 2021**
- End Date: **December 30, 2025**
- Total Sales Records: **17,378**

### 2. Revenue Breakdown by Year and Month

Analyzed Connecticut sales revenue by year and month to identify changes in sales performance over time.

**Annual Revenue Results:**

| Year | Total Revenue |
| ---- | ------------: |
| 2022 |   $399,252.57 |
| 2023 |   $553,024.43 |
| 2024 |   $555,941.61 |
| 2025 |   $884,003.83 |

Revenue increased significantly in 2025, growing approximately 59% compared with 2024.

### 3. Connecticut vs. East Region Revenue

Compared Connecticut's total in-store sales revenue with the combined revenue generated by the East region.

**Results:**

| Territory / Region | Total Revenue |
| ------------------ | ------------: |
| Connecticut        | $2,392,222.44 |
| East Region        | $6,723,039.53 |

Connecticut accounts for approximately **35.6% of East region revenue**, making it a significant contributor to regional sales.

### 4. Monthly Transactions and Average Sales by Product Category

Analyzed the number of sales records and average sales amount by month and product category.

**Category Results:**

| Product Category         | Sales Records | Average Sales Amount |
| ------------------------ | ------------: | -------------------: |
| Technology & Accessories |         3,665 |              $466.00 |
| Textbooks                |         2,361 |              $173.01 |
| Apparel and Merchandise  |         3,396 |               $31.56 |
| Art Supplies             |         2,840 |               $32.06 |
| Books (General)          |         1,397 |               $29.06 |
| Stationery and Supplies  |         3,719 |                $9.96 |

Technology & Accessories had the highest average sales amount, while Stationery and Supplies had the highest number of sales records.

### 5. Store Sales Performance Ranking

Ranked Connecticut store locations based on total in-store sales revenue.

**Results:**

| Rank | Store Location    | Total Revenue |
| ---: | ----------------- | ------------: |
|    1 | New London        |   $331,116.76 |
|    2 | Bridgeport        |   $322,100.07 |
|    3 | Hartford          |   $309,592.06 |
|    4 | Waterbury         |   $295,651.17 |
|    5 | New Haven         |   $286,779.43 |
|    6 | Litchfield County |   $286,217.15 |
|    7 | Darien            |   $283,455.62 |
|    8 | Old Saybrook      |   $277,310.18 |

New London generated the highest revenue, while Old Saybrook recorded the lowest.

### 6. Business Recommendations

Based on my analysis, I recommend that Emporium focus on increasing sales in Connecticut by continuing to prioritize Technology & Accessories and Textbooks, which generate most of the territory's revenue. The company should also look at what successful stores like New London and Bridgeport are doing and find ways to improve sales at lower-performing locations like Old Saybrook. Since revenue increased significantly in 2025, management should identify what contributed to that growth and find ways to keep it going.
